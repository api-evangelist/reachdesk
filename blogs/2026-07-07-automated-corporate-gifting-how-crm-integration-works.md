Connecting a gifting platform directly to a CRM lets teams trigger personalized gift sends automatically based on real buyer behavior, such as a lead score threshold or a stalled deal in Salesforce or HubSpot. This removes manual gifting workflows and creates measurable revenue impact across sales, marketing, and customer success.
